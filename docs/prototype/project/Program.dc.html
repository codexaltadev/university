Overview

Embark on a transformative journey with the MBA program at Daffodil International University — a world-class education experience for aspiring leaders and entrepreneurs.

Program objective

To produce graduates with profound knowledge in the relevant disciplines of business, and with the competence to work as independent entrepreneurs.

Credit & duration

A minimum of 40 credit hours. Minimum duration 1 year; the degree must be completed within 5 years.

Program variants

For both fresh graduates and professionals looking to enhance their leadership and management skills.

Eligibility

Students with a minimum GPA of 2.50 in SSC and HSC, and graduation from any group — BBA, B.Com (Hons), BA, B.Sc, M.Com, MA, M.Sc, MBBS or an equivalent background — may apply for admission.
